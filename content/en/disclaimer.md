@intro
This project is experimental. Our early findings are interesting, but they should be treated as strictly preliminary.

@points
- **The accuracy isn't guaranteed.** The models are only moderately accurate, and their predictions can vary a lot.
- **The results are suggestive at best.** They're meant for educational interest, not as objective fact.
- **Much of the training data is Malaysian.** A large part of it comes from Malaysian PetFinder, so the claims are less certain when applied to Taiwan.
- **This is not policy.** The tool is **not** meant to be read as policy, and is **not** a sufficient basis for real, consequential decisions about shelter operations or animal care.
